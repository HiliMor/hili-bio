01 / About

Curiosity gets it started.
Reality makes it better.

I spent seven years in intelligence analysis, where incomplete information is normal and judgment matters. Now I build software the same way: start with the real problem, learn what I need, and keep going until it works outside the demo.

My work connects development with visual design, from booking systems to editorial websites and AI-assisted brand imagery. Tests, users, and careful iteration help me bring the pieces together.
